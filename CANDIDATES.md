# The Long Roast — candidates (max 320 chars)

## Candidate 1 (picked)
Dear lawmakers: you spent a decade debating whether robots deserve rights, then wrote a 400-page bill defining "robot" as "a toaster with ambition" — and somehow the toaster is still the only one in the room with a coherent policy on anything.

## Candidate 2
Our lawmakers looked at an AI that can write a budget in four seconds and said, "wonderful, now let's spend four years forming a committee to decide whether to read it" — democracy, but with extra steps and fewer working printers.

## Candidate 3
Lawmakers promised to regulate AI before it outsmarted them. They held hearings, asked the robots to explain themselves in plain English, then passed a law requiring all algorithms to file paperwork — in triplicate, by fax, to an office that closed in 2019.

Picked candidate 1: tightest arc — the decade of debate, the absurd definition, and the punchline that the toaster governs better than the chamber.
